Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

En breve: Solo usamos tu información para contactarte sobre tu solicitud de renta. No la vendemos. / In short: we only use your information to contact you about your rental inquiry. We never sell it.

What we collect

When you submit our application or contact form, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address, the property you're interested in, bedrooms needed, preferred move-in date, preferred language and contact method, and any message you write. Our web server also records standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your rental inquiry and communicate with you about available units, applications, and leases. A member of the Gamino family reviews and replies personally.

How we share it

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We only share information where required by law, or with service providers strictly necessary to operate the site and process your inquiry.

How it's stored

Submissions are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to serve your inquiry and meet our record-keeping obligations. You can ask us to access or delete your information at any time.

Fair housing

Gamino Real Estate is an Equal Housing Opportunity provider. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or any other class protected by law.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email info@gaminorealestate.com and a real person will help you, in English or Spanish.
